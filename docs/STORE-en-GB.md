# Store copy draft (not submission approved)

Proposed title: PrepTrove – Emergency Kit & Family Planner
Tagline: Prepared today. Ready for tomorrow.

Organise emergency essentials and household plans in one private place. Create kits, track supplies and recorded expiry dates, save important contacts, and review practical checklists. Saved records are available offline without an account.

Voice recognition and spoken feedback depend on device services and may require internet access. PrepTrove is a planning companion, not an emergency alert service or replacement for official advice. Completing a checklist does not guarantee safety.

Do not publish until release gates are closed. Premium and worldwide localisation claims are deliberately omitted from this draft. Price and title-length validation remain pending.
