# Publisher review before submission

Prepared 3 November 2026 for O.Bankole, United Kingdom. Purchases remain disabled. This review does not certify legal compliance or accept Amazon's export declaration.

## Confirmed application behavior

The implementation stores planner records locally, disables Android automatic app backup, contains no publisher analytics or ads, and requires no PrepTrove account. Exports are user initiated. Optional speech services, document providers, external websites and Amazon distribution services have their own processing. The privacy and deletion pages describe those boundaries. No cached emergency recommendations remain.

## Support correspondence proposal

The public support address supplied by the publisher is banksmi@mail.com. The following is proposed final-notice wording for the publisher to approve and operate:

> When you contact support, O.Bankole uses your email address, message and any information you choose to send to respond and investigate the enquiry. Providing this information is optional; core device-only planning does not require contacting support. Do not send private household plans, health information, passwords, recordings or backup files.
>
> For ordinary support correspondence, the proposed lawful basis is legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and maintaining the app. Correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the enquiry, deal with relevant follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Support messages are not used for marketing or automated decisions about you.
>
> You may contact banksmi@mail.com to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of support information, or object to its use, subject to applicable law. Device-only records are managed through the app's deletion/export controls; the publisher cannot retrieve or remotely erase them. You may also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your local supervisory authority.

This is a proposal, not evidence of an existing mailbox retention or deletion process. The publisher must confirm that the mailbox is monitored and that this handling matches actual practice. No verification email has been sent on the publisher's behalf.

## Items still requiring publisher assessment

- Confirm the support purpose, lawful basis and retention criteria above and the ability to handle rights requests. The balancing considerations are minimal optional correspondence, an expected response to a user-initiated enquiry, no marketing, and avoidance of sensitive attachments. This record does not establish an approved legitimate-interests assessment.
- Review the actual mail.com account/service terms, recipients, processing locations and any applicable international-transfer arrangements. No UK-only hosting, processor agreement, transfer safeguard or provider deletion guarantee has been verified. Do not state those as established facts in the public notice.
- Approve the final public privacy/deletion text and the console's audience, content/privacy declarations, territories and disabled-purchase scope. Remove review labels only after that approval.
- Amazon requires a certification covering imports, exports and encryption in all authorized territories. The checkbox remains unchecked. The unsigned APK contains PTB backup encryption; ordinary use of platform crypto does not by itself establish the publisher's export classification or legal certification.
