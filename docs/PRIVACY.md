# PrepTrove privacy notice — development draft

PrepTrove stores the records you enter in its private application storage on this device. It does not require an account and contains no ads or publisher-added analytics. It does not access your location or device contacts. Android automatic application backup is disabled.

Microphone access is optional and is used only when you start voice recognition. Recognition is provided by a device service that may process audio over the internet under its own terms. PrepTrove does not retain microphone recordings. Spoken plan content may be heard by nearby people. TTS may use installed provider services.

Notifications are optional. Due reminders are periodically checked on the device. Timing depends on operating system restrictions.

Exports are created only when you choose a destination. PDF, CSV and JSON files are not encrypted. PTB backup files use password-protected encryption; the password cannot be recovered. Contacts and family plans are excluded unless you select them; notes in other categories may also contain personal information. Destination providers may sync files according to their settings. PrepTrove does not upload records on its own.

Delete all saved records from Settings, or remove app data using device settings. Previously exported copies remain at their chosen destinations. PrepTrove does not claim secure erasure from flash storage.

Publisher/controller name supplied by the owner: **O.Bankole**. Public support contact: **banksmi@mail.com**. Publisher country, jurisdiction-appropriate notices, support retention and final legal review remain unconfirmed.

Supply photos are copied into private, backup-excluded device storage after size/dimension validation and JPEG re-encoding. The original EXIF metadata is not copied. Removing a photo from a record unlinks it; the private file may still be used by duplicate records/templates. Delete all saved records also removes stored photo files and completion history. Exports do not contain photo pixels or completion history.

Optional app lock uses the device screen-lock credential and blocks the app UI after backgrounding. It does not add database encryption. The app does not read or store the device credential.

Amazon SDK purchase communication is disabled in the unconfigured development build. If enabled for a future commercial build, Amazon processes store account/transaction data; the app locally caches an account identifier and entitlement state. Household records are not sent by the billing adapter. Store SDK disclosures and the final privacy notice require release review.

## Amazon distribution services

Amazon documents that its distribution process re-signs and wraps uploaded Android apps. The distributed app communicates with the Amazon Appstore client, including when Amazon DRM is not enabled. Amazon describes store analytics, policy evaluation and aggregated reporting among the wrapper purposes. These platform services are distinct from publisher-added analytics, which PrepTrove does not integrate. Review Amazon's applicable terms and privacy information alongside the final publisher notice. Source checked 30 October 2026: [Understanding Amazon Appstore submission](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/app-submission/understanding-submission.html).
