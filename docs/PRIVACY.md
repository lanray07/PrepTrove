# PrepTrove privacy notice — development draft

PrepTrove stores the records you enter in its private application storage on this device. It does not require an account and contains no ads or analytics. It does not access your location or device contacts. Android automatic application backup is disabled.

Microphone access is optional and is used only when you start voice recognition. Recognition is provided by a device service that may process audio over the internet under its own terms. PrepTrove does not retain microphone recordings. Spoken plan content may be heard by nearby people. TTS may use installed provider services.

Notifications are optional. Due reminders are periodically checked on the device. Timing depends on operating system restrictions.

Exports are created only when you choose a destination. Files are not encrypted. Contacts and family plans are excluded unless you select them; notes in other categories may also contain personal information. Destination providers may sync files according to their settings. PrepTrove does not upload records on its own.

Delete all saved records from Settings, or remove app data using device settings. Previously exported copies remain at their chosen destinations. PrepTrove does not claim secure erasure from flash storage.

Before public release: add the publisher's legal name, real support contact, jurisdiction-appropriate notices and hosted policy URL, and obtain legal review.
