# PrepTrove privacy notice

Updated: 3 November 2026. Publisher/controller: O.Bankole, United Kingdom. Contact: banksmi@mail.com.

PrepTrove stores the records you enter in its private application storage on this device. It does not require an account and contains no ads or publisher-added analytics. It does not request device location or read your device address book. Household locations and contact details you choose to enter stay in your local records. Android automatic application backup is disabled. Optional accessibility/support notes may contain health information you choose to enter; the app does not read health-platform, fitness-sensor or biometric records. Supply purchase dates are manually entered planning information; this release does not process app-store purchases or payment-account data.

Microphone access is optional and is used only when you start voice recognition. Recognition is provided by a device service that may process audio over the internet under its own terms. PrepTrove does not retain microphone recordings. Spoken plan content may be heard by nearby people. TTS may use installed provider services.

Reminder notifications are off until you enable them in PrepTrove Settings. You can disable them again without deleting saved reminders. The choice stays on this device and is not enabled by a restored backup. Due reminders are checked periodically, with additional best-effort refreshes after reminder changes. Notices contain a generic title and due count, without household names, notes, locations or record titles. Empty or disabled notices are cleared. Timing and availability depend on operating system restrictions and notification settings.

Exports are created only when you choose a destination. PDF, CSV and JSON files are not encrypted. PTB backup files use password-protected encryption; the password cannot be recovered. Contacts and family plans are excluded unless you select them; notes in other categories may also contain personal information. Destination providers may sync files according to their settings. PrepTrove does not upload records on its own.

Delete all saved records from Settings, or remove app data using device settings. Previously exported copies remain at their chosen destinations. PrepTrove does not claim secure erasure from flash storage.

Publisher/controller: **O.Bankole**, **United Kingdom**. Contact: **banksmi@mail.com**.

Supply photos are copied into private, backup-excluded device storage after size/dimension validation and JPEG re-encoding. The original EXIF metadata is not copied. Removing a photo from a record unlinks it; the private file may still be used by duplicate records/templates. Delete all saved records also removes stored photo files and completion history. Exports do not contain photo pixels. Completion-history events are included only when you select the history option for JSON/PTB backups; their earlier record titles may contain personal details.

Optional app lock uses the device screen-lock credential and blocks the app UI after backgrounding. It does not add database encryption. The app does not read or store the device credential.

Amazon SDK purchase communication is disabled in this release. If enabled for a future commercial build, Amazon processes store account/transaction data; the app locally caches an account identifier and entitlement state. Household records are not sent by the billing adapter. Any future purchase-enabled release will update the relevant disclosures.

## Optional support correspondence

When you contact support, O.Bankole uses your email address, message and any information you choose to send to respond and investigate the enquiry. Providing this information is optional; core device-only planning does not require contacting support. Do not send private household plans, health information, passwords, recordings or backup files.

For ordinary support correspondence, the lawful basis is legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and maintaining the app. Correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the enquiry, deal with relevant follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Support messages are not used for marketing or automated decisions about you.

You may contact banksmi@mail.com to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of support information, or object to its use, subject to applicable law. Device-only records are managed through the app's deletion/export controls; the publisher cannot retrieve or remotely erase them. You may also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your local supervisory authority.

The app does not read your email inbox. Support messages and the address you use are collected only when you choose to contact the publisher. Support email is processed using mail.com. Review its [privacy information](https://www.mail.com/company/privacypolicy/) for provider processing, recipients and locations. The email service operates under its own terms and privacy information; this notice does not promise UK-only storage or a particular provider deletion guarantee. Device-only planning does not require sending support email.

## Amazon distribution services

Amazon documents that its distribution process re-signs and wraps uploaded Android apps. The distributed app communicates with the Amazon Appstore client, including when Amazon DRM is not enabled. Amazon describes store analytics, policy evaluation and aggregated reporting among the wrapper purposes. These platform services are distinct from publisher-added analytics, which PrepTrove does not integrate. Review Amazon's applicable terms and privacy information alongside this publisher notice. Source checked 30 October 2026: [Understanding Amazon Appstore submission](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/app-submission/understanding-submission.html).

## Official-source directory

The optional country/region choice for official-source links is stored on this device independently of app language. PrepTrove does not use location permission or infer your physical location for this directory. Choosing Open official website sends the selected public URL to your browser. The external publisher and browser may process network/device information under their own policies; PrepTrove does not download, translate or cache the external advice. The directory preference is a separate app setting, reset by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
