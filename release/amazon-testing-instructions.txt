No login, account or test credentials are required. Purchases are intentionally disabled; no paid entitlement is required for the available planning tools.

Complete or skip onboarding. Create a kit, add a supply and assign it to the kit. Edit quantity, unit, storage location and an optional date. Save a household plan and a manually entered contact. Reopen the app to verify local persistence.

Create a reminder and explicitly enable notifications in Settings if desired. Notification delivery is best-effort and depends on OS settings. Completion, snooze, pause and completion history are available.

Settings offers PDF, CSV, JSON and password-protected PTB exports through the device document picker. Sensitive contacts/plans and completion history are optional selections. Photos are excluded from backups. JSON/PTB restore creates copies and preserves existing records. Keep the chosen PTB password; it cannot be recovered.

The interface supports 22 locale packs, including regional variants. Device language is detected, and Settings allows a manual language choice. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Dates, quantities and commands use the selected app language; existing user-entered records are not translated.

Voice recognition and text-to-speech depend on installed device services and their supported languages. If recognition is absent or microphone permission is declined, use the typed-command field. Typed-command examples appear in the selected app language. In English, try: add bottled water; what supplies are expiring soon; show my contacts. Review drafts before saving. Recognition may require internet access; the app does not retain recordings.

Optional app lock uses the device screen-lock credential. It is not a separate PrepTrove PIN and does not add database encryption. A secure device credential is needed before enabling it.

Saved planning records work offline. PrepTrove is not an emergency-alert service and does not provide cached emergency recommendations. Reference offers a country/region picker and opens an official publisher's website in an external browser. The directory choice is independent of app language and persists locally; it does not detect physical location. External websites require internet access and are not downloaded or translated by PrepTrove. Completing a checklist does not guarantee safety.
